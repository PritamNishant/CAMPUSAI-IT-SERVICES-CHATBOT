Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CampusFix AI

CampusFix AI can help with common campus IT issues including Wi-Fi connectivity problems, login and password issues, software installation and configuration, printer problems, network connectivity, and basic system configuration. For complex or specialized issues, the system will create a support ticket for your campus IT team.

Yes! If CampusFix AI cannot resolve your issue through troubleshooting, it will help you create a detailed support ticket. The ticket includes all the diagnostic information gathered during your conversation, making it easier for IT staff to assist you.

Absolutely. When a ticket is created, you'll receive a ticket ID that you can use to track the status of your request. You'll be notified of updates and can communicate with IT staff through the ticketing system.

If CampusFix AI cannot resolve your issue, it will guide you through creating a support ticket. The ticket is automatically categorized and assigned to the appropriate IT team based on your problem. All the information from your conversation with Fixie is included to help IT staff understand your issue faster.
